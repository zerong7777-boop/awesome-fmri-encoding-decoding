# Figures

These figures summarize the repository's scope and modeling paradigms. They are conceptual illustrations; the heatmaps, cortical highlights, and scatterplots are not experimental results.

| Figure | High-resolution PNG | Dimensions | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Unified landscape | [Download PNG](Figure1_fMRI_Unified_Landscape.png) | 8250 × 3750 px | 300 DPI |
| 2. Modeling taxonomy | [Download PNG](Figure2_fMRI_Modeling_Taxonomy.png) | 8250 × 3750 px | 300 DPI |

The PNGs are rendered from the two-slide [editable PowerPoint source](ppt/fMRI_Figures_Editable.pptx). Text, icons, heatmaps, and arrows remain editable in the source. Its 27.5 × 12.5 inch canvas produces the listed pixel dimensions at 300 DPI. The PNGs also contain physical-resolution metadata.

The main README displays the figures at the available page width. Open or download a PNG to inspect it at full resolution. When updating either figure, edit its corresponding slide and re-export at the same size and resolution.
